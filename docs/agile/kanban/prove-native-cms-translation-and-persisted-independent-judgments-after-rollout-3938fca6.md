# Prove native CMS translation and persisted independent judgments after rollout

## Outcome

After the qualified Services deployment, a controlled fresh CMS document produces
an actual Spanish translation through Knoxx's deployed provider, and independent
translation judgments persist against its exact revision. Deliver the native IDs,
source/provider/runtime custody and revision-bound receipts for the epic and the
separate content/publication proof; a prior isolated bundle is not this result.

Depends on completed EC1.03 (`03a057e4-3098-4fe7-a5b8-95168f45ca96`)
only. Before that deployment is admitted, the separately estimated
[predeployment translation qualification](qualify-selected-native-knoxx-translation-and-sme-decisions-before-deployment-5eae82d9.md)
(`60e85d88-3960-449f-8376-10e75eae82d9`) supplies accepted exact-source translation

its broader scope and entry conditions; this is a bounded rollout proof, not its

## Scope

Use the actual native CMS/API with a controlled English source, terminology and
Spanish locale. Retain canonical document/segment IDs and revisions; invoke real
Knoxx translation using effective direct-host Ollama `gemma4:e2b`, thinking off.
Record genuine completion and actual independent translation/SME judgment
identities, full inputs and persisted outcomes. A model catalog or configuration
value cannot prove those calls. Freeze the resulting Spanish candidate revision
and hand it to the content-proof story without borrowing that story's judgment,
whole-publication decision or publication receipt.

For this project-scoped rollout, freeze the authenticated organization/tenant ID
and nonblank project identity along with the document, garden, source and target
locales, source revision, translated-output revision and content digest. Carry
that same scope through the source, completed translation, every judgment and
approval, and the handoff. The owning receipt uses `:translation/org-id` and
`:translation/project`; the owning approval uses `:review/org-id` and
`:review/project`, attributed from the validated receipt/server context rather
than trusted from a caller's approval request. Retain any split manifest,
candidate-set and per-split review lineage the actual operation emits. A legacy
nil project or otherwise matching revision from another scope cannot qualify
this named-project proof.

Completion requires the owning boundary's current accepted translation decision
for these exact output bytes, not merely the existence of historical judgment
records. Read the complete persisted judgment history and its effective result,
then obtain/read the distinct revision-bound approval. Bind its native identity,
principal, disposition and currentness to the scoped completed translation and
its independent judgments before handing the candidate to publication.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Independently read back scope-qualified source, output, judgment and
  approval identities from the owning storage/API. Organization/tenant, project,
  document, garden, both locales, both revisions and content digest agree across
  the complete join; client-supplied scope declarations alone cannot pass.
- [ ] Actual deployed application/controller/caller/run/attempt and provider route,
  model/effective thinking mode bind the real translation operation and its complete
  input/output. Prior receipts, synthetic returns or provider fallback cannot pass.
- [ ] The intended Spanish document/segments are complete; actual translation
  judgment(s) persist with distinct assessor identities and exact candidate revision.
  Missing judgments, wrong locale, omitted segments or stale decisions remain failures.
- [ ] The owning API/storage readback proves canonical history and judgment IDs,
  not only rendered text. Translation acceptance does not imply content-product
  acceptance or whole-publication approval.
- [ ] Handoff records the immutable Spanish candidate/revision and its receipts.
  Any later content change invalidates this proof's join until independently requalified.
  Existing English/German publications and the deployed content root are preserved.
- [ ] The current effective translation judgments and owning approval accept the
  exact current Spanish output. Where the split-review path is used, retain the
  complete history and its effective `approve` / `:approved` outcome for every
  required split; `reject`, `needs_edit`, deferred/pending work or a missing
  judgment cannot complete this card. The final approval's `:review/state` is
  `:approved`, and the owning current-approval join binds source locale,
  translated-output revision and content digest to the current completed receipt.
- [ ] The immutable handoff names that same organization/tenant, project, full
  source/output identity, complete judgment references and current accepted
  translation approval. A fresh owning read immediately before handoff refuses
  changed source/output, superseded or rejected effective judgments, missing
  approval or a scope mismatch; historical approvals remain inspectable history.

## Verification and estimate

Retain the genuine predeployment translation vertical test result separately.
Run a bounded actual postdeployment CMS/API journey and inspect persisted decisions,
history and provider effect receipts. Exercise the owning stale-revision, missing-
judgment and wrong-locale refusal controls on the qualified owned fixture; production
uses only the controlled authorized document. Record actual commands/URLs, IDs,
hashes and outcomes without credentials/raw private sources in public evidence.

On the qualified owned fixture, seed two projects in one organization and a
second organization with deliberately equal document/revision/locale labels.
Attempt to reuse each other scope's source, completed output, judgment, approval
and handoff; every cross-project and cross-organization join must refuse with no
foreign publication effect. Also approve output A, regenerate B for the same
source, and show that A's approval cannot qualify B; exercise later rejection,
needs-edit/pending disposition and missing required judgment. Keep these negative
controls separate from the authorized live document. Preserve the exact observed
IDs, history and refusals instead of manufacturing matching scope fields.

Three points assumes the deployed native translation/review paths already exist
and are qualified. Any implementation defect is a separate owning repair, not
scope silently added to this proof. Layout, drafting, content judgment,
whole-publication approval, reconciliation and browser publication belong to the
content-proof story. No live call is claimed by this draft.

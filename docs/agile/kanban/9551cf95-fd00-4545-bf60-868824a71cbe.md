# Extract Clio as the portable immutable-fact substrate

Clio is not one of the letters. It sits underneath them: things happened, here
are the immutable facts and their causal relationships, and current state is
derived from them.

The root policy already states the rule — ledgers record immutable facts, and
projections, caches, database rows, UI state and provider responses never become
semantic authority merely because they exist. The rule has no implementation.

Two specimens already exist in this workspace and disagree with each other:

- `.ημ/kanban-events/ledger.edn` — real append-only events with `:event/type`,
  `:event/id`, `:event/time`, `:payload`, and `:causal/root`.
- `.ημ/receipts.edn` — append-only records keyed on `:kind` with `:manifest`,
  `:refs`, `:tests`, and prose `:note`.

Both are honest append-only logs. Neither has a deterministic fold, so nothing
is derived from either; they are read by humans and greps.

## Acceptance

- One portable `.cljc` event shape covering both specimens, or an explicit
  statement of why they stay separate.
- A deterministic fold exists and is tested: same facts in, same projection out,
  order-independent where the semantics allow it and explicitly ordered where
  they do not.
- Projections are disposable. Deleting every projection and refolding from facts
  reproduces them exactly. Verified by test, not asserted.
- Causal links (`:causal/root`, `:event/causes`) survive the extraction and mean
  something a fold can use.
- Replay is idempotent — one of the laws Knoxx paid for and should not be
  rediscovered.
- All provenance stays physically under `.ημ/`.

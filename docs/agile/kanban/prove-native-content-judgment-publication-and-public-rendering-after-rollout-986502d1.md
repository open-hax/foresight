## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real native drafting and content-product judgment persist with actual complete
  inputs, provider effect, distinct assessor and revision identities. Borrowed
  translation judgments or declared model settings cannot establish this effect.
- [ ] The exact Spanish candidate has its own persisted content/product decision,
  separate whole-publication approval and actual reconciliation receipt. Missing,
  stale, wrong-authority or mixed-revision inputs refuse the owning operation.
- [ ] The public manifest/artifact and browser render the exact approved Spanish
  revision, locale and intended representation. A local fixture, direct file copy
  or isolated old Spanish bundle supplies no publication effect proof.
- [ ] Existing English/German publications and content-root ownership survive with
  fresh before/after manifest and public readback. Deployment, translation,
  content judgment and publication receipts remain separately inspectable and
  join only through their exact identities.
- [ ] A current accepted translation decision for the exact scoped Spanish
  source/output identity is independently read before whole-publication approval
  and freshly checked at reconciliation/admission. Supersession, rejected or
  unfinished effective translation judgment, missing required judgment/approval,
  wrong digest or cross-project/tenant evidence refuses publication without a
  new materialization; existing publications remain preserved.
- [ ] Independently persisted drafting/content judgments, whole-publication
  decision and the emitted reconciliation receipt bind the same authorized
  organization/tenant and project as their respective source/output. The receipt
  retains its actual trigger, publication, concrete revision, target and operation
  correlation, and its durable storage record carries the scope-qualified join
  to the approved output/digest and application/run/attempt evidence.
- [ ] The actual publication-effect receipt is committed to the configured
  durable sink and independently read by identity from that storage after an
  authorized publisher-process restart. A returned response or journal GET alone
  cannot qualify durability. Its immutable bytes/hash and scope/correlation are
  unchanged after restart and after exceeding the volatile journal's 200-entry
  bound on the qualified owned fixture; retained history is not rewritten or
  silently dropped. The sink's declared policy retains these closure records
  append-only without a TTL or bounded eviction, so they remain inspectable after
  epic completion. Missing persistence, failed emission, absent readback or a
  retention policy that can discard the closure evidence prevents closure.

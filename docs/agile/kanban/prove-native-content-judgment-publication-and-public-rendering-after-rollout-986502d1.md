- [ ] Real native drafting and content-product judgment persist with actual complete
  inputs, provider effect, distinct assessor and revision identities. Borrowed

- [ ] The exact Spanish candidate has its own persisted content/product decision,
  separate whole-publication approval and actual reconciliation receipt. Missing,
  stale, wrong-authority or mixed-revision inputs refuse the owning operation.
- [ ] The public manifest/artifact and browser render the exact approved Spanish
  revision, locale and intended representation. A local fixture, direct file copy
  or isolated old Spanish bundle supplies no publication effect proof.
- [ ] Existing English/German publications and content-root ownership survive with
  fresh before/after manifest and public readback. Deployment, translation,
  content judgment and publication receipts remain separately inspectable and
  join only through their exact identities.

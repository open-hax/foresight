# Reconcile fork staging and promotion PRs

## Outcome

Turn a qualified personal-fork merge into exact-SHA staging proof and idempotent upstream/Foresight PRs.

## Scope

- Reuse fork-dev-origins and its mapped fork/network identity; preserve protected main history.
- Only an admitted qualified feature merge submits artifacts to trusted Services orchestration. Candidate code never receives deployment/signing credentials or a writable token, including same-repository PRs. Gate implementation/configuration and App identity are independent of the candidate.
- Successful staging is required before creating/reusing the in-org promotion branch/PR and affected Foresight integration PR.
- Preserve existing Proxx staging-to-main behavior until a separately reviewed migration; production is a separate evidence gate.
- Sync upstream merge history back through a protected fork PR; use immutable receipt-bound commits rather than moving branch tips.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A fixture fork merge deploys its exact SHA to staging and records public readiness and behavior evidence.
- [ ] Failed or absent staging opens neither promotion nor integration PR.
- [ ] Duplicate/out-of-order events reuse jobs and PRs; a delayed old event never substitutes newer fork main.
- [ ] Each affected child gitlink is exactly the commit covered by its successful trusted staging receipt and is reachable from its approved destination. Record receipt ID/hash/issuer and upstream PR; reject silent substitution of an org merge SHA. Requalify the new Foresight head.
- [ ] Protected fork sync preserves ancestry without force pushes. Its admitted upstream-sync purpose is terminal: replay produces no staging, promotion or integration; unknown purpose rejects, and editable labels/prefixes supply no authority.
- [ ] Required org admission rejects manually opened promotion PRs missing exact staging proof. Both mains reject direct writes/bypass, and strict checks or a merge queue recheck a changed base/merge candidate.

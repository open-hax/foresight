## Context

The eta-mu Antigravity review provider is proposed in open-hax/eta-mu#364.
Foresight has22 actionable independent direct members plus the root. Inventory-only
consolidation inputs and nested package policy remain outside rollout authority.

## Outcome

Every member gets an immutable thin caller for the shared Gemini3.8 Flash review
workflow, preserving its existing deterministic checks and package-manager policy.

## Scope

Pure member selection/rendering laws; explicit --all/--only CLI plan/check/apply;
independent child worktrees and PRs; root caller and a durable rollout census.

## Non-goals

No gitlink updates before approved child reachability and required staging proof.
No merge, native approval identity, branch-protection changes, or secrets forwarding.

## Acceptance criteria

- Enumerate root plus all22 actionable members from foresight.project/sources.
- Refuse consolidation inputs, undeclared/nested paths, mutable refs and absent checkouts.
- Pin workflow and runtime to full immutable commits; pin gemini-3.8-flash-high.
- Require explicit target selection before writes; never overwrite unfamiliar workflows.
- Use read-only tokens without forwarding signing/deployment credentials.
- Report missing worker/authentication as unavailable; do not turn artifact prep into a passing review.
- Preserve independent ownership, unrelated dirt, and every existing check.

## Verification

Red/green member and rendering tests; adapter preflight and idempotence checks;
clj-kondo zero warnings; rendered caller validation across all selected worktrees.
Native reviews/CI and persistent-worker qualification remain separate evidence.

## Risks

Consumer PRs depend on reviewed eta-mu runtime/workflow commits and authenticated
worker provisioning. Cross-organization runner-group access requires a suitable
enterprise restriction or another reviewed hosting adapter.

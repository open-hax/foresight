# Finish or explicitly declare the Katamorph schema purification

The plan of record was a behaviour-preserving **move** of `katamorph.schema`
from `src/cljs` to `src/cljc`, keeping the public namespace and fixing the
CLJS-only `catch :default` in `coerce`.

What actually landed is a **new parallel core**: `katamorph.schema.core`,
`.kind`, `.registry`, `.validation` under `src/cljc/`, while the original
802-line `src/cljs/katamorph/schema.cljs` remains and still carries its own
`schema-for` and `validate`. Both are on the classpath — `:paths` lists
`src/cljc` and `src/cljs`.

So the seam that was supposed to end duplication currently produces two
implementations of schema resolution and validation. That is the exact
condition the consolidation exists to end, and it is the kind of thing the
root jscpd baseline should be able to see.

## Acceptance

- A decision is recorded, and it is one of:
  - `schema.cljs` becomes a thin CLJS shim over the portable core; or
  - the split is intentional and the boundary between the two is documented,
    naming which one is authority for what.
- Whatever is decided, only one implementation of registry lookup and
  validation is semantic authority.
- The public namespace consumers depend on keeps working, with the change
  covered by a suite that runs (see c697c36a-2247-47f5-a7a6-8e64150e0deb).

## Boundary

Katamorph is an independently owned repository under `.gitmodules`. This work is
a PR in `open-hax/katamorph`, not a root edit, and it must not reach into its
package-manager policy or nested packages.

---
Alpha is already a live consumer: `alpha.law.markdown.profile` and `.facet`
require `katamorph.schema.core`, while `alpha.law.artifact` still calls Malli
directly. That inconsistency is downstream of this decision, so this card
sequences ahead of b114c341-e1eb-4f99-8152-b50cc2aaa88a.

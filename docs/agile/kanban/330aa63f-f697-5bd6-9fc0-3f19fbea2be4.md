# Host persistent MiMo and Kimi review workers

## Outcome

Keep model execution alive independently of an Actions waiter, with immutable job inputs and durable evidence.

## Scope

- Eta-mu owns admission/publication; Sol owns execution; Services owns an isolated service slot at the declared Knoxx ingress.
- Accept a signed dispatch of repository, PR number, base/head SHA, pinned contracts, evidence-manifest digest and requested model. Reject ambiguous or moving revisions.
- Candidate checkout and model tools run without App signing or deployment credentials. The trusted publisher uses its own identity and rechecks head/policy before publication.
- Persist queued/running/terminal records and artifacts before acknowledging transitions. Resume/reconcile after a process restart without duplicate publication.
- Expose status/findings/cancel/retry through Knoxx contracts with Axxium principal, installation, repository and actor authorization or an explicit operator scope. Authentication alone grants no other repository's jobs; read, cancel and retry permissions are distinct. Quota/auth/network failure is a visible terminal result or scheduled backoff, never an approval.
- MiMo and Kimi are independent requested workers. Register both; one qualifying approval may satisfy model quorum while all required deterministic evidence remains mandatory.
- Actions dispatch is the first trigger; direct webhook response is a later slice.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Real MiMo and Kimi jobs publish truthful, exact-head review results from the server.
- [ ] Killing/restarting the worker and ending the Actions waiter do not lose an admitted job or fabricate success.
- [ ] Duplicate dispatch, stale-head publication, quota failure and missing terminal evidence fixtures reject unsafe admission.
- [ ] Fixed service configuration, pinned source/image, state paths, readiness and rollback are reviewed and recorded in Services.
- [ ] Live API calls prove permitted read/cancel/retry, anonymous and cross-principal/repository denial. Cancellation is idempotent; an authorized retry gets a new immutable attempt ID/event and cannot alter historical outcomes. A document or placeholder does not pass.

### Proposed canary admission and budget

- Use a small public versioned fixture containing a planted actionable defect
  and a benign control. A deterministic versioned evaluator checks expected
  findings and control behavior; model prose cannot grade itself. The fixture
  uses the admitted restricted tool profile, without private candidate content.
- Compare against a retained explicitly accepted baseline only when route,
  endpoint/profile, client/runtime, prompt, tools, requested/observed control,
  configuration and fixture/evaluator revisions all match. A changed scope
  requires separate baseline acceptance, not a behavioral-change assertion.
- Before first use after a route/endpoint/profile/client/runtime/tool/prompt/control/configuration
  or fixture/evaluator change, run the admitted canary cycle; for an unchanged
  scope, run at most once per 24 hours. Deduplicate cycles through existing job
  admission and persist the last attempt before submission, including failures;
  an unavailable cycle cannot trigger repeated polls or renew its baseline.
- Proposed cycle limit: at most two small probe attempts in total, including
  retries, each at most 4,096 input tokens, 1,024 output tokens and 120 seconds;
  at most 240 seconds per cycle. One canary executes at a time and shares a
  proposed MiMo/Kimi reviewer-hosting budget of two model jobs globally/one per
  reviewer, including their canaries. This scoped proposal is distinct from the
  [generic evidence-worker graph](../../notes/design/eta-mu-github-evidence-workflow.edn)'s
  proposed six model-backed workers; neither is an operational hosting limit.
  Enforce supported token/time caps before submission; an unsupported cap blocks
  probe admission. These are proposed local admission limits, not attested
  reasoning budgets.
- Use only the explicitly admitted cheap MiMo route or existing Kimi plan route
  with its verified minimum supported control. No paid fallback, automatic
  model/account rebind, billing overage or unbounded orchestration. No canary is
  authorized by this planning document alone.
